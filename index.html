There Is Something
About You

I sometimes wonder if you realise just how extraordinarily good you are.

Not because you're perfect. Not because you never have difficult days or moments when everything feels like too much.

It's because even with all of that, there is still so much goodness in you.

You care about people. You notice things that other people overlook. You remember the tiny details. You worry about the people you love. You give so much of yourself without even realising how much it means.

And somehow, you have brought that same warmth into my life.

You make ordinary moments feel special. You make difficult days a little easier. Sometimes you don't even have to do anything. Just knowing that you are there changes something.

I think that's why I call you sunshine in my head.

Not because you're happy every second of every day, but because even when you're dealing with your own storms, there is something about you that makes the world around you feel warmer.

And I hope you know how rare that is.

I love the person you are. I love the way your mind works, the way your heart works, the way you care about little things that most people wouldn't even notice.

I love all those tiny pieces of you that make you, simply, you.

And if I could give you one thing in return for all the warmth you've brought into my life, it would be the ability to see yourself through my eyes for just one day.

Maybe then you'd understand why I look at you and think that somehow I got incredibly lucky.

You are not just someone I love. You are one of the brightest things that ever happened to my life.

So whenever you doubt yourself, whenever life becomes heavy, or whenever you forget just how amazing you are...

Please remember this: somewhere in this world, there is someone looking at you and thinking,

"How did I get so lucky to love her?"

You are my favourite ray of sunshine. ☀️
