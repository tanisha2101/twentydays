A Little Letter For A Rainy Day

My Nushi, There will be gloomy days. There will be bright ones too. There will be days when everything feels easy, and days when life feels a little heavier than usual. But however the day looks, however the weather feels, I hope you always remember this: You never have to be at your perfect self to be loved. On the shiny days, on the rainy ones, on the days when everything feels right and on the days when nothing seems to. I will always want to be there for you, beside you through all of them. Because life isn't made beautiful by having perfect days. It's made beautiful by having someone who makes even the rainy ones worth remembering. ❤️
