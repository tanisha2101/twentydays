A LITTLE LETTER FOR YOU

To My Favourite Foodie ❤️

I think one of my favourite things about us is how easily food became one of our little languages.

We can turn a simple meal into an entire adventure. We can talk about what we want to eat, decide what we absolutely need to order, and somehow still end up saying, “Should we get something else?”

And honestly, I wouldn't have it any other way.
