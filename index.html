A LITTLE NOTE 🌅

My World
Comes Back To You

Sometimes I think my world has quietly learned to revolve around you.

Not in some grand, dramatic way. It happened slowly. In little pieces.

In the things I look forward to. In the people I want to tell things to. In the moments I wish you were beside me. In the places I want to explore with you. In the future I catch myself imagining.

Somewhere along the way, loving you stopped being something I simply do.

It became a part of who I am.

I think I have built so many little pieces of myself around the idea of always loving you.

And honestly, I am grateful for that.

Grateful that you exist.

Grateful that in this enormous world, somehow our lives crossed.

Grateful for every ordinary day that became special simply because you were part of it.

Grateful for every laugh, every conversation, every walk, every stupid little moment, every memory that I know I will carry for a very long time.

I don't think I could ever properly explain what your existence has done to my life.

You have given me the best reason to look forward to tomorrow.

Best person to dream with.

Best place that feels like home.

You.

And maybe that is why I love sunsets.

Because after an entire day of everything — the noise, the rush, the stress, the chaos — the world eventually becomes quiet.

The light softens. Everything slows down. And for a little while, nothing needs to be anything more than exactly what it is.

That's what loving you feels like to me.

Coming home.

So tonight, I just want you to know one thing.

I am unbelievably grateful that you are in my life.

Grateful that I get to know you. Grateful that I get to love you. Grateful that I get to make memories with you.

And no matter how many sunsets I see, I think a part of me will always be amazed that my favourite person is real.

My world may be enormous...

but somehow, it always finds its way back to you.

With all my love,
Bui ❤️
