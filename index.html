TONIGHT'S LETTER

To The Girl With The Kindest Heart

There is something about you that I don't think I tell you enough.

You care.

You care about people, about the things they feel, about the little details they mention once and probably forget about themselves. You notice when someone isn't okay. You remember the smallest things. You worry. You protect. You give pieces of yourself to the people you love without even making a big deal about it.

And I have been lucky enough to experience that kindness myself.

You have protected me from parts of the world that felt too heavy, sometimes without even realising that you were doing it.

There have been so many moments where you cared for me, looked after me, understood something I couldn't explain, or simply made life feel a little easier.

And those little things mean more to me than I think you will ever know.

I think that's what makes you so incredibly special. Not just one grand thing. Not just one perfect quality. It's the thousand tiny ways in which your heart chooses to care.

Sometimes I imagine our future and I see a home filled with laughter, ridiculous little arguments, sleepy mornings, celebrations, ordinary evenings and a family that knows just how lucky they are to have you.

And I can't help but think that our children would have the most beautiful childhood simply because they would have you.

They would have someone who notices when they're quiet. Someone who remembers their favourite little things. Someone who protects them when the world feels frightening and teaches them kindness when it doesn't.

And honestly, I think they would be the most adorable little humans in the world.

Because they would carry a little bit of your heart with them.

If this world ever feels too big or too cruel, I hope you always remember that there is someone who sees the goodness in you, notices everything you do, and feels incredibly lucky to love you.

You are my favourite kind of magic.

And if I could choose a world to live in again,
I'd choose the one where I get to find you. ❤️
