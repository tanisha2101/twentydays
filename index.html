A Note Between Two Foodies ❤️

I think one of my favourite things about us is how easily something as simple as food becomes a memory with you. A plate of momos. A random café. Walking around looking for something we both want to eat. Arguing over what to order. Stealing a bite from each other's plate. Somehow, those little things become some of my favourite parts of us. I don't just love the big moments with you. I love the ordinary ones too. The silly ones. The hungry ones. The “let's just try this place” ones. Because when it is with you, even a simple meal feels like something I want to remember. So here's to all the food we haven't eaten yet, all the places we haven't discovered, and all the little meals that are still waiting to become memories. ❤️

— Your forever food partner 🥢
