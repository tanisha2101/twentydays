A little letter for you

To the person who feels like home

There are some people who enter your life and somehow make it feel a little warmer. A little softer. A little more like home. You are that person for me. I don't think home has always been a place. Sometimes it is a laugh you know by heart. Sometimes it is a hand you want to hold. Sometimes it is a person beside whom you can finally let the whole world become quiet. And for me, so much of that feeling has become you. I love the little things about us. The conversations, the stupid jokes, the walks, the food, the dates, the difficult days, the beautiful ones and all the ordinary moments in between. Because somehow, even the ordinary feels special when it is ours. I hope you always know that you have a place where you don't have to be strong. You don't have to have everything figured out. You can simply be you. And you will always be loved there. I am grateful for every version of you that I have had the privilege of knowing, and for every version of you that I am yet to meet. Maybe that is what home really means. Not a perfect place. Just a place where your heart knows it belongs.

♡ One last thing ♡

If life ever becomes too loud, I hope you remember that you never have to carry everything alone.
