UNTIL YOUR BIRTHDAY
17 DAYS
CHAPTER XVIII
❦ A LETTER I COULD NEVER FINISH ❦

My Mou,

There are some things I don't know how to say when you're standing in front of me. So tonight, I am leaving them here instead. I don't know when we will talk again. I don't know what tomorrow will feel like. And I don't know what this particular chapter of our story is going to look like. But there is one thing I have never been confused about. I love you. I have loved you through the easy days, through the ridiculous ones, through the days where everything felt effortless, and through the days where understanding each other felt impossibly difficult. And somewhere along the way, loving you stopped being just one part of my life. It became woven into it. You became a thousand little things I didn't know I would miss until they weren't there. A person I could tell everything to. A person whose happiness could change the entire mood of my day. A person I could look at and think, somehow, I got lucky enough to know her.

I don't think I have ever properly thanked you for everything you have been in my life. For the memories you gave me. For the laughter. For the ordinary days that became special simply because you were there. For letting me know a version of you that the rest of the world doesn't get to know. For becoming my person in ways neither of us probably planned. You gave me pieces of your life, and without even realizing it, you left pieces of yourself in mine. And I will always be grateful for that.

Maybe that's why today hurts so much. Because when someone means this much to you, even a little distance can feel enormous. But I don't want this letter to ask anything from you. You don't have to answer it. You don't have to fix anything tonight. You don't even have to know what you feel right now. I just wanted one place where I could be completely honest. I loved you. I love you. And whatever happens from here, that love will always be one of the truest things I have ever felt.

And if life ever takes us somewhere far away from this moment, I hope it is kind enough to leave a little road between us. Because no matter how many chapters life writes after this one, some part of me will always hope that somewhere, somehow, we find our way back to the same page. Not because I expect it. Not because I am asking for it. Just because if there is one person in this enormous world I would always want to find my way back to... it would be you.

I don't know what the ending of our story is. Maybe neither of us is supposed to know yet. But I know this much: I will always be grateful that, for a while, our lives found each other. And if I could go back and read the beginning again, I would still choose every page that led me to you.

Always with love,
Your Bui

But tonight, I wanted you to know that the gratitude I have for having known you isn't dependent on how perfect things are. You mattered to me on the happiest days. You matter to me on the difficult ones too. And somewhere in this very strange, beautiful life of ours, I hope there is always a little possibility that our paths cross again. Some people become memories. Some become home. You will always be something much more precious than a passing chapter to me.
