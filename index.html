TONIGHT'S LETTER

When The World Gets Quiet

I know there are days when everything feels like a little too much. Days when the stress piles up, when your mind refuses to slow down, and when being strong starts feeling like something you have to do rather than something you simply are.

And I want you to know that I see you.

I see how hard you try. I see everything you carry, everything you handle, and everything you still manage to do even when you're tired. And I couldn't be more proud of you for the person you are.

But when the day finally ends, when the world becomes quiet and you don't have to be anything for anyone, I hope you remember that you have somewhere to rest.

If you ever come to me tired, I won't ask you to be strong. I'll simply open my arms and let you rest there.

You can bring me your difficult days. You can bring me your worries, your silence, your tiredness, your tears, or simply nothing at all. You never have to make yourself smaller or hide the parts of you that are hurting.

I want to be the place where you can finally breathe. The place where you don't have to fight the whole world by yourself.

Because loving you isn't something I want to do only when everything is beautiful. I want to love you through the quiet nights, the difficult mornings, the uncertain days and every little moment in between.

You are so incredibly precious to me. More than I think words will ever be able to explain.

I love you more than I know how to put into words. And somehow, every day, I find myself loving you a little more than the day before.

So when tonight gets quiet,
come rest for a while. ❤️
